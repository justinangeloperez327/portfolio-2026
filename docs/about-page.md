# About Page

## Purpose

The About page explains the developer behind the work without turning the
portfolio into a biography or duplicating the resume.

## Structure

1. Positioning statement
2. Development background and current direction
3. Areas of focus
4. Working and learning approach
5. Link back into engineering case studies

## Content strategy

The page connects application-development experience with current framework,
language, frontend, and architecture work. It presents those areas as an
evolving engineering practice rather than claiming equivalent depth in every
technology.

## Focus areas

- Application engineering
- Framework design
- Developer experience
- Systems learning

## Editorial constraints

- Do not use skill percentages.
- Do not create a technology-logo wall.
- Do not inflate experiments into professional production experience.
- Keep personal information limited to details relevant to the professional
  portfolio.
- Let the Resume page carry chronological employment and education details.
- Let the Engineering page carry detailed technical philosophy.
