# Homepage Hero

## Purpose

The hero establishes identity, engineering focus, location, and the visual
language of the portfolio without behaving like a marketing landing page.

## Content hierarchy

1. Engineering context
2. Name
3. Positioning statement
4. Selected work and engineering paths
5. Portfolio/location metadata
6. Sumi-e brush composition

## Composition

The desktop hero uses the twelve-column editorial grid. The name occupies the
dominant upper-left field while the positioning statement sits in a narrower
right-hand column. The ink mark occupies a lower visual field and is balanced
by quiet location metadata.

On mobile, all content follows a natural reading order and the brush mark
becomes a restrained separator rather than a dominant graphic.

## Constraints

- No animated headline.
- No rotating job titles.
- No typewriter effect.
- No parallax.
- No large portrait in the initial composition.
- No generic "Hello, I'm..." introduction.
- The brush mark remains decorative and cannot carry information.
- Primary actions are text-forward links rather than oversized buttons.
