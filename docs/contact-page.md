# Contact Page

## Purpose

The Contact page provides a direct professional contact path without requiring
a backend, database, third-party form processor, or unnecessary client-side
JavaScript.

## Contact channels

- Email
- GitHub

Email is the primary professional contact channel. GitHub provides a route to
the public engineering work.

## Why there is no contact form

A form would currently add infrastructure, validation, spam prevention,
privacy, and operational responsibilities without improving the basic contact
workflow enough to justify them.

A mail link keeps the interaction transparent and lets the visitor use their
own email client.

A form can be introduced later if there is evidence that it materially
improves contact conversion or a backend becomes useful for other reasons.

## Suggested first-message context

Visitors are encouraged to include:

- the problem;
- relevant constraints;
- expected outcome;
- technical context.

## Privacy

The page does not collect visitor information directly. Analytics and any
future form processing remain separate concerns and should be documented when
introduced.
